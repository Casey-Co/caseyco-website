Additional options

Support beyond the core plan.

Health Spending Accounts

Available upon request to add on. Flexible coverage for CRA medical expenses where the core plan may not be enough.

Can range between $250–$2,000/year per employee.

Protect Your Volunteers

Many non-profits rely on volunteers to ensure their operation is smooth. Our plan offers an add on to your benefit plan for Accidental Death and Dismemberment to cover those who encounter the unexpected while volunteering.
